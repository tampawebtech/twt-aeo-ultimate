The Problem It Solves

Traditional SEO doesn't cut it anymore.

When someone asks an AI search engine a question, it doesn't rank ten blue links — it picks one answer. TWT AEO Ultimate audits every signal that determines whether that answer comes from your site.

What AI engines need from your site

  • Complete, accurate structured schema markup
  • Clear authorship and E-E-A-T signals
  • Entity identity — who you are, where you are, what you do
  • Fresh, timestamped content
  • Social graph coverage (Open Graph, Twitter/X Cards)
  • Crawlability by AI bots, not just traditional search spiders

What most sites are missing

  • FAQPage schema on pages that have Q&A content
  • Author Person schema with credentials and sameAs links
  • LocalBusiness schema with verified NAP data
  • Missing or malformed Open Graph tags
  • Schema conflicts between installed plugins
  • No visible content freshness signal

TWT AEO Ultimate runs a continuous audit of all these signals — automatically, on every page — and tells you exactly what to fix and why.

Modular by Design

Every feature is opt-in. Nothing runs unless you turn it on.

TWT AEO Ultimate ships with over 20 modules covering every AEO discipline. Enable exactly what your site needs — nothing more.

E-E-A-T & Author Signals

🪪 Author Entity

Full E-E-A-T profiles per author: credentials, certifications, social sameAs links. Outputs Person schema with EducationalOccupationalCredential. Fields live directly inside Users → Profile — no separate UI to learn.

👤 Author Schema

Detects whether your theme displays author information front-facing and adds Author schema only when it's appropriate and missing — no false positives, no schema on pages that don't need it.

🆔 Author Box

Audits whether your site has a visible author box and matching schema. Flags missing bio or avatar and recommends a fix. AI engines weigh author credibility heavily — this makes it visible.

💬 Author Hover Cards

When a visitor hovers an author link, a lightweight card surfaces: bio, job title, certifications, expertise tags, and social profiles. Credibility signals visible to humans and readable by crawlers.

📅 Last Updated Badge

Prepends a visible "Last Updated: [date]" line to blog posts when the content has been revised. A simple, explicit freshness signal that AI models actively look for when deciding which source to cite.

Schema

🔍 Schema Advisor

Evaluates existing schema against page intent (homepage, service page, blog post, location, product). Flags genuinely missing or wrong schema — no warnings for things that are intentionally absent.

❓ FAQ Detector

Scans every page for accordion blocks, FAQ sections, and Q&A patterns. If FAQPage schema is missing, it flags it — and can add it automatically. One of the highest-value schema types for AI citations.

🔧 Service Detector

Classifies service-oriented pages and flags those missing Service schema. Rescans automatically on post save so your schema always reflects your latest content.

📍 Contact Detector

Audits contact pages for Organization, PostalAddress, and ContactPoint schema. Flags missing entity data and consolidation problems that confuse AI entity resolution.

📰 Google News

Generates a Google News sitemap, outputs NewsArticle JSON-LD with full Author Entity integration, and adds a per-post metabox for article section, keywords, and sitemap exclusion.

⚠️ Schema Conflict Detector

Finds schema conflicts between TWT AEO Ultimate and other installed plugins — Yoast, Rank Math, AIOSEO, WooCommerce, and others. Identifies the exact duplicated types and explains how to resolve them.

Social Graph

🔗 Social Graph Manager

Unified Open Graph and Twitter/X Card management across every page. Scans for missing OG fields, lets you edit og:title, og:description, og:image, twitter:card, and twitter:creator from a single modal. Auto-fills twitter:creator from Author Entity data.

🕵️ OG Detector

Audits every page for Open Graph coverage and shows gaps at a glance. When AI models share or summarize your content, OG data is how they pull the title, image, and description — gaps here mean your brand loses control of how it's represented.

Local SEO & Indexing

📌 Local Pack

Build complete LocalBusiness schema with a predictive business-type picker, opening hours, service area, and geo coordinates. Audits NAP consistency against Google Knowledge Graph, Google Business Profile, and Bing Maps — entity mismatch is one of the most common reasons AI engines distrust local businesses.

⚡ IndexNow

Instantly notifies Bing, Yandex, and all IndexNow-compatible engines the moment you publish or update content. Generates and hosts your API verification key automatically — no file upload, no manual steps.

🗺️ Sitemap Generator

Generates a clean XML sitemap at /aeo-sitemap.xml, configurable per post type with change frequency and priority. Cache invalidates on content changes. Detects if Yoast, Rank Math, or AIOSEO already provides one and warns you before enabling a duplicate.

WooCommerce

🛒 WooCommerce Schema

Scans products for schema presence, classifies them as physical or service, and flags those missing Product or Service schema. Rescans automatically on save.

🏪 Google Merchant Center Sync

Compares your WooCommerce catalog against your live GMC feed. Surfaces price and availability mismatches, GTIN/MPN/SKU gaps, sync latency, and rejection error codes directly from the Content API — no switching tabs.

🔵 Bing Merchant Center Sync

Same depth of catalog auditing for Bing/Microsoft Advertising via the Shopping Content API. Item-level disapproval codes surfaced directly inside WordPress.

AI & Content

📢 PR Bridge AI

Detects press release intent in posts, transforms them into AP-style copy using Claude AI, and distributes to wire services (EIN Presswire, EasyPRWire). A Media Report tracks distribution status for every release.

🤖 AI Ready

Configures your site for AI crawler accessibility and manages AI API keys (Claude, OpenAI). On WordPress 7.0, keys integrate natively with the Connectors API under Settings → Connectors — no duplicate key management.

🎛️ Command Center

Manage your Google OAuth connection, Bing Webmaster integration, and other external platform links from one place inside WordPress admin.

TWT AEO Ultimate — Full Changelog
=================================

= 2.28.1 =

* **Fixed: engines keyed after the allocation was saved were never asked (`TWTAEO_Visibility_Store::effective_engines()`).** The allocation stored the engines ticked at save time, and an engine whose key arrived later rendered unticked, so every run skipped it (aeoultimate.com ran ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude only, with Grok, DeepSeek and Muse keyed). Saving now also records which engines had keys (`engines_seen`); an engine keyed after that is ticked, one left unticked while keyed stays off. Allocations saved before this count every keyed engine not ticked as new. The intro text now lists all eight engines.
* **New: your own questions and rewording (`TWTAEO_Visibility::post_custom_questions()`, `merge_saved_questions()`).** A "Your own questions" box above the question list takes one question per line (up to 50, 300 characters each), stored with source `custom` and an id from the words, so the same question compares run to run. They are asked on every run on top of the allocation counts, first so a capped run keeps them, and count in the checks arithmetic. Each built question has Edit (source `merchant`, id unchanged) and "Use the original wording", which restores the template text kept beside an edit (`template_text`).
* **New: "Is this you?" (`TWTAEO_Visibility_Brands::classify_cited()`, `page_key()`, `handles()`).** The business's names in web-address form (`aeo-ultimate`, `twt-aeo-ultimate`) are matched against cited addresses. On a shared platform with the name in the account slot (first two path segments; on LinkedIn posts only the author before `_`), the page is a candidate and the results board asks once: Yes adds the page, cut after the segment carrying the name, to the company's owned pages (`claim_page()`) and re-scores every kept run; No is remembered (`not_me`) and never asked again.
* **New: pages about you.** A cited address carrying the name anywhere else (someblog.com/reviews/aeo-ultimate-review), and unanswered candidates, fold into one light-blue "pages about you" slice in share of voice instead of competitor slices (`share_of_voice()` takes an optional `$about` context); an answer that also cited an unrelated page on the same host keeps that host as a competitor. A list under the donuts shows each page, its count and engines.
* **New: re-scoring without new calls (`TWTAEO_Visibility_Store::rescore_runs()`).** Every answered check of every kept real run is judged again from its stored answer and cited links against what counts as you today; accuracy marks stay. Runs on "Yes, that's me" and on saving What counts as you.
* **Improved: owned-page matching (`property_matches()`).** wordpress.org language copies (`en-gb.wordpress.org`, `jv.wordpress.org`) match a registered wordpress.org page; LinkedIn posts (`/posts/<handle>_…`) match a registered `/company/`, `/showcase/` or `/in/` page with the same handle. `MAX_URLS` raised from 12 to 25.
* **Changed: more shared hosts need a full address.** `apps.shopify.com`, `wix.com`, `chromewebstore.google.com` and the wordpress.org language subdomains are refused as bare hosts.
* **Fixed: `%d check` / `%d checks` was missing from the 2.28.0 POT** although translate.wordpress.org's scan found it; the POT is regenerated for 2.28.1.

= 2.28.0 =

* **Readme:** AI Visibility and the external-services list name Muse (Meta AI) and DeepSeek (DeepSeek marked as working and still being tested), with bullets for the search location and "gave no sources"; the RAG Engine section mentions the Trash/unpublished restore on Search Placement and the business-topic grouping in Next questions; 2.28.0 changelog and upgrade notice added. Stable tag stays 2.27.0 until release.
* **Fixed: RAG Engine matched long questions on words every page shares (`TWTAEO_RAG_Signals::distinctive_term()`).** On a spindle site "spindle", "CNC" and "repair" are in every document, so "What is Hybrid Ceramic vs Steel Bearings in CNC Spindles?" got Hurco catalogue pages and three router-repair questions got the same router chart. A question of three or more subject words now needs its most distinctive word in the passage: the one found in the fewest indexed passages across pages and documents ("ceramic", "Heian"), counted once per word per page load.
* **Fixed: follow-ups that say "it" or "they" lost their subject.** "What electrical, cooling, and pneumatic requirements does it have?" after "Is VEM ECR11A Spindle good?" was matched to an HSD manual. A follow-up that points back now takes the model number, or else the most distinctive word, of the question it followed.
* **New: "Questions about your business" in AI Citations → Next questions (`TWTAEO_RAG_Signals::business_topic()`).** Most follow-ups after "Is X a legitimate store?" or "What is X's return policy?" ask about the business itself (turnaround, warranty, rush service, shipping, reviews, pricing, which brands you work on, quotes), and brochure passages were offered for them. Follow-ups that name the business, or that follow a company question and ask about "them" or one of those topics, are now grouped by topic with the closest page you have, and never matched to documents. The page shown for each topic is the site's own page for it, by web address or title (warranty, shipping, FAQ, contact, about, terms, reviews…), not a product page that shares a few words; with none, the table says no page covers the topic yet. On the first Atlanta Precision Spindles run with follow-ups, 86 of 120 distinct follow-ups were about the business: turnaround 18, quotes and contact 10, shipping 10, then reviews, rush service, brands and warranty 9 each. A follow-up also inherits the model number of the question it followed whenever it names none of its own, pronoun or not ("What are the typical runout, power, speed, and torque specifications?" after the VEM ECR11A question).
* **Changed: the review request is now an offer of help (`TWTAEO_Review_Notice`).** Three days after install (was seven), the banner on the plugin's own screens offers help instead of asking for a review: "Need a hand with TWT AEO Ultimate? … Ask in the support forum. We read and answer every thread", with **Ask a question** (the wordpress.org support forum), All good, thanks, and Maybe later. A new option key (`twtaeo_help_offer_dismissed`) shows it once even to sites that hid the old review request.
* **Fixed: approving, unapproving or deleting a review on the Reviews page could stop at "The link you followed has expired".** The review banner listened for the same `twtaeo_review_action` link parameter as the Reviews page and ran its own nonce check on those clicks; the banner now uses `twtaeo_help_action`. Uninstall now also removes `twtaeo_installed_at`, `twtaeo_review_dismissed` and `twtaeo_help_offer_dismissed`.
* **Changed: follow-up turns are counted where the run is counted.** With Follow the conversation on, the run list said "375 checks" while the progress bar had ended at 720. The run list now reads "375 checks + 345 follow-up turns" (the headline figures still count first answers only), and the live progress line says how the growing total splits: "433/582 (465 first questions + 117 follow-up turns so far)". Run summaries carry `turns`; summaries saved before are recounted once.
* **Changed: RAG Engine Overview wording.** The Your pages button reads **Re-index pages** (was Rebuild, which read as if it rebuilt AI Citations too), and the AI citation check row says a new AI Visibility run shows up on AI Citations by itself as soon as it has answers, with nothing to rebuild.
* **New: "Gave no sources" in AI Visibility (`TWTAEO_Page_AI_Visibility::gave_no_sources()`).** "Not cited" covered two different outcomes: a competitor was cited in your place, or the engine linked to no one at all. An answer that did not cite you and cites nobody else is now marked **gave no sources**, the verdicts themselves unchanged: each engine's card counts them ("76 of 125 answers gave no sources"), the answer drawer says "Gave no sources. This answer links to no one, so no one was cited in your place." (first answers and follow-up turns), AI Citations adds "Muse gave no sources" beside "engines cited … instead", and the CSV has a `sources_given` column (yes / no; blank when unanswered). Works on runs already recorded.
* **Fixed: Muse (Meta AI) could almost never score "cited" (`TWTAEO_Visibility_Engines::named_results()`).** In the first full run with Muse (Atlanta Precision Spindles, 125 questions) Muse was cited on 12 against ~80 for ChatGPT and Gemini, and 151 of its 211 answers recorded no sources. Muse's API answers usually carry no `url_citation` annotations and no links in the text, only the search results it read (a live 3,800-character answer naming several shops had none). When an answer has no annotations, a search result now counts as cited when the answer names its site: the domain's name spelled out in the text, spaces and punctuation ignored ("HS Spindles" → hsspindles.com, "Atlanta Precision Spindles" → atlantaprecisionspindles.com). Names under five letters are skipped, and a site named after an everyday word (spindles.co.nz) counts only where the word stands as a name of its own, capitalised and not the end of a longer name ("HS Spindles"). Results Muse read but never named do not count. Runs already recorded keep their verdicts (their search results were not stored).
* **New: GPT-6.1 Sol in the OpenAI model list (Settings → AI models).** `gpt-6.1-sol`, near-Astra quality at Sol pricing ($2 / $10 per million tokens); GPT-6 Sol stays as "previous Sol". **Fixed:** GPT-6.1 accepts reasoning effort low, medium, high, xhigh and max only, so `TWTAEO_AI_Models::openai_reasoning_effort()` now sends `low` for GPT-6.1 and newer (it sent `none`, which GPT-6.1 refuses; GPT-6.0 keeps `none`). The recommended OpenAI model is unchanged (GPT-6 Luna).
* **Fixed: RAG Engine offered a catalogue's table of contents as the answer (`TWTAEO_RAG_Signals::is_contents_page()`).** "Hurco DCX models" matched page 5 of the Hurco catalogue, a contents list ("VM Series 6 VMX Series 8 …") that names every series and answers none. Passages with five or more "Series/Chapter/Section N" entries, or a "Contents" heading followed by six or more page numbers, are no longer offered, on Search Placement and AI Citations alike (the office-list filter now covers AI Citations too, through the same matching).
* **Fixed: AI Visibility questions built from headings written in the site's own voice (`TWTAEO_Visibility_Questions::heading_question()`).** "Hurco VMX Models We Service" became "Can you explain Hurco VMX Models We Service?", which no buyer asks. A "{X} models/spindles/brands we service/repair/carry…" heading now becomes "What Hurco VMX models are there?"; other headings about we/us/our/you ("Why Choose Us") or calls to action and section labels (Contact…, Get a quote, FAQs, Overview) are skipped for the post's next heading.
* **New: "You used to have a page for this" on RAG Engine Search Placement (`TWTAEO_RAG_Signals::former_page()`).** When Google or Bing still ranks the site for a search, no published page carries it in its title or web address, and a page that does is in the Trash, back to draft, pending or private, the search gets its own card, whether or not a document answers it: the page, where it is, a **Restore from Trash** link (or Open it to publish), and why putting it back at its old address beats starting a new page, or redirecting it if it was removed on purpose. Only a page named for the search counts: its words together and in order in the title or web address ("VibroControl 6000 Vibration Monitor", "GMN HCS 160 …" for "gmn hcs 160"), or the model number it names; "GMN HCS 160 Spindle Repair: When Precision Starts to Drift" is not a page for "precision spindle". No card when Google reports a live page or the home page ranking for the search, and one card per search however many ranking rows it has. Trashed slugs lose their `__trashed` suffix; a page renamed since is found by its old address. Permanently deleted pages cannot be found. Office lists are also dropped when they open a longer passage, or when a passage carries eight or more phone numbers and emails.
* **Fixed: RAG Engine Search Placement suggested office address lists (`TWTAEO_RAG_Signals`, from the Atlanta Precision Spindles site).** "new precision spindles" was answered with a brochure's list of branch offices, phones and emails, because it contains "Precision Spindles". Passages that are mostly contact details (four or more phone numbers or emails, and fewer than 12 other words per one) are no longer offered; a checklist with the shop's phone at the top still is (`is_contact_block()`). Also: a search whose only model number is a plain number ("vibrocontrol 6000") now needs the name beside it in the passage too, not just "6000 rpm", and the no-page note names "VIBROCONTROL 6000" instead of "6000"; the same passage no longer shows twice when a document was uploaded twice; towns in the Local Pack service area now mark a search as looking for a local business, like the business's own city ("cnc routing atlanta").
* **New: "Don't close this page" warning while documents upload (RAG Engine → Documents, `documents.js`).** From the moment an upload starts until the last file is sent, a bold warning sits above the progress line, and the browser asks before the page is closed or left. Both clear once the upload finishes; reading and indexing afterwards carry on without the page.
* **New: Muse (Meta AI) joins AI Visibility** (engine `meta`, label "Muse (Meta AI)", key `api_meta` / `TWTAEO_META_KEY`, Settings → AI API Keys). Meta Model API (public preview), Responses format at `api.meta.ai/v1/responses` with a Bearer key: search grounding is documented for the Responses API only, not Chat Completions. Model `muse-spark-1.3`, the docs' recommended model; never the `-contributor` variants, which let Meta train on prompts and completions (here, a client's questions and personas). Tool `web_search` with `user_location` `{country, region, city, timezone}` (Meta's shape: no `type`), retried without it on a 400; `include: ["web_search_call.results"]`. Cited URLs are the `url_citation` annotations on `output_text`; searched URLs are the `web_search_call` results. Follow-ups use the same Responses endpoint with JSON-schema `text.format` (retried without on 400). The engine id matches the existing `meta.ai` AI-referrer id, so Meta AI referral visits line up with Muse citations; `meta.ai` added to AI referral detection. `api.meta.ai` added to allowed probe hosts. Verified: request body and parser against the documented response shape (intercepted), live endpoint with a dummy key (HTTP 401, recognised as a key error), and a live two-question run from Tampa with a real key.
  * **Background mode (`TWTAEO_Visibility_Engines::ASYNC_ENGINES`, `submit_async()` / `poll_async()`).** Muse answered in 50–90 s per question in live testing, even with searches capped, which would hold every other engine's checks behind it. A run now submits a Muse question with `background: true` (about 1 s, returns a response id) and moves on; later steps poll `GET /v1/responses/{id}?include[]=web_search_call.results` (at most one poll per step, each ticket at most every 5 s) and score the answer exactly like any other (`record_answer()`, shared with answers that come back at once). Tickets live in the run's allocation JSON (`pending`). When everything else is done the step returns `waiting` and the page says how many answers are still out; the background worker finishes the run if the page is closed, and the run is marked done only when nothing is pending. A ticket still unanswered after 10 minutes, or one the provider marks failed, is recorded as unavailable with its reason; a 5xx or network error on a poll is retried at the next poll. A rejected key at submission is handled like any engine (the rest of that engine's questions are skipped). Journey mode's next turn is queued when the background answer arrives.
  * **Search cap:** `max_tool_calls: 3` (as Claude's `max_uses`). Uncapped, Muse ran ~15 searches per question (~55k input tokens); capped, ~6–9k (about 3¢ a question at standard-tier prices).
  * **Answer text is the final message only.** Muse writes a message before each search ("I'll search for…"); earlier messages are dropped, and citations are taken from the final message.
  * The background worker now raises the PHP time limit to 120 s per step (as the AJAX step handler does) and holds its lock for 180 s. `TWTAEO_Visibility_Http::get()` added (same host allow-list and key redaction as `post()`).
* **New: search location for AI Visibility runs (`TWTAEO_Visibility_Location`, "Search from" on the run form).** The consumer apps know roughly where the person asking is and grounded results change with it; an API call carries no such signal, so without a location a check searched from wherever the provider's infrastructure is. Each run now carries one location `{ country (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2), region, city, lat?, lng?, source }`, copied onto the run (in the allocation JSON, no table change) like the persona.
  * **Default when nothing is chosen ("Automatic"):** the Local Pack address for a local business (city, state, country, coordinates), else the WooCommerce store country for a store (country only: it sells beyond its town), else the site language's country (en_US → United States), else none. The form names the source ("your Local Pack address"). "No location" is the only way to search from nowhere. One location per run: to see several markets or branches, run once for each.
  * **Picker:** Automatic, No location, English-speaking markets first (US, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, India, Singapore, South Africa), then every country (WooCommerce's list when active, else a bundled ISO list). Region is a list for those markets (WooCommerce's state lists when active; bundled lists for all nine) and typed elsewhere; city is typed. Region codes are stored as names ("FL" → "Florida").
  * **Two layers per check:** every engine gets a context line in the system instructions beside the persona ("User location: The user is in Tampa, Florida, United States."), never in the question. Claude, ChatGPT and DeepSeek also get `user_location` `{type: approximate, country, region, city, timezone}` on the web search tool (time zone from a US state map or a single-zone country), and Perplexity gets `tools[{type: web_search, user_location: {country, region, city, latitude, longitude}}]`. Gemini, Grok and Le Chat document no search location, so the board calls their location approximate. A 400 on the request with a location is retried once without it (DeepSeek has not documented the field); `tools` records `user_location` only when it was accepted.
  * **Comparisons:** trend and "vs previous run" compare only runs with the same persona and the same location (`compare_key()`); run labels and the run header show "from Tampa, Florida, United States". Runs before 2.28.0 had no location, so the first located run starts a new trend line instead of showing a jump.
  * **CSV export:** `search_country`, `search_region`, `search_city` per run and `search_location` per check (`search` = the engine's search was given the location, `told` = only the assistant was told it).
* **New: DeepSeek joins AI Visibility (status: working, still in testing; the readme says so)** (engine `deepseek`, label "DeepSeek", key `api_deepseek` / `TWTAEO_DEEPSEEK_KEY`, Settings → AI API Keys). Asked through DeepSeek's Anthropic-compatible Messages API (`api.deepseek.com/anthropic/v1/messages`, `x-api-key`), the interface DeepSeek documents web search for; its OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions API documents none, and without search an engine can never cite. Model `deepseek-flash` (DeepSeek's primary model, V4.1 Flash since 2026-09-10; `deepseek-chat` / `deepseek-reasoner` were retired 2026-07-24). Search tool `web_search_20250305` with `max_uses` 3. Claude's request and reply parsing are shared (`anthropic_messages()`): answer text, text-block citations as cited URLs, `web_search_tool_result` URLs as searched URLs. Follow-up questions use the same endpoint without JSON-schema output (not documented there) and are read by `parse_follow_ups()`. A rejected key (HTTP 401 "Authentication Fails") is recognised by `is_key_error()`, so a run skips DeepSeek instead of failing every question. DeepSeek documents its search as a separate model call that summarises the results, so an answer may name sources as links in its text instead of citation objects: when none came back but a search did, links written in the answer count as cited only if their site is among the search results (a link the model made up never counts). `api.deepseek.com` added to the allowed probe hosts; `chat.deepseek.com` counted as an AI referrer. Verified against the live endpoint with a dummy key (HTTP 401, key redacted); answers with a funded key not yet verified.
* **New: FAQ schema drift detection (`TWTAEO_FAQ_Detector::drift()`).** Stored FAQPage schema is a saved copy of the page's Q&A; before this, a rescan only asked whether FAQPage existed, so a rewritten page kept publishing its old questions and answers. The stored FAQ is now compared with the Q&A extracted from the page by content (normalized text, never modified dates): `in_sync`, `changed` (questions added or removed, answers reworded), `orphaned` (the page shows no FAQ content but the schema is still published, against Google's FAQ rules) or `unverifiable` (FAQ-style content the extractor cannot read, such as accordions). Product FAQs are excluded; they are managed as their own list.
* **New: FAQ fingerprint (`_twtaeo_faq_fingerprint`).** Every FAQ the plugin generates from page content goes through `save_generated()` / `remember_generated()` (Add FAQ Schema, Generate All, Autopilot, Schema Autofill) and is fingerprinted, so a stored FAQ is known as `generated` (untouched since the plugin wrote it), `edited` (changed by hand) or `unknown` (saved before 2.27.1). A pre-2.27.1 FAQ that matches its page exactly is adopted as generated.
* **New: automatic sync on save.** When a page is saved, a generated, untouched FAQ that no longer matches is rebuilt from the page. Hand-edited and pre-2.27.1 FAQs are only flagged, and an orphaned FAQ is never removed automatically.
* **New: Schema Detector → FAQ tab.** "FAQ Schema Out of Date" summary card; per-row status with what changed (counts plus the questions no longer on the page); "Update from page" (asks before replacing a hand-edited FAQ) and "Remove FAQ schema" for orphaned FAQs (new `twtaeo_faq_remove_schema` AJAX action; removes only the plugin's own stored FAQPage). Orphaned pages are listed even though they no longer have FAQ content.
* **New: editor metabox** shows "FAQ schema out of date with the page" or "FAQ section removed" with a Review link to the FAQ tab.
* **New: Markdown twins at `/page.md`** (`TWTAEO_AI_Ready::route_md_suffix()`, `request` filter), the llms.txt proposal's convention, alongside `Accept: text/markdown` and `?aeo_format=markdown`. Resolved with `url_to_postid()` to the page's own query, so it works under any pretty permalink structure; `/index.md` is the front page; plain permalinks fall back to `?aeo_format=markdown` (`markdown_url()`). A `.md` address with no Markdown version returns a plain-text 404, never a themed page. Behind the existing URL fallback setting.
* **New: Markdown discovery.** Every singular page and the front page carry `<link rel="alternate" type="text/markdown">` in the head and the same RFC 8288 `Link` header, whichever plugin serves llms.txt.
* **New: YAML frontmatter** on every Markdown twin: title, url, description (manual excerpt, else the SEO description from this plugin, Yoast, Rank Math, AIOSEO or SEOPress; values with template variables skipped), author, published, modified, language, site. The title heading is added only when the page has no H1 of its own.
* **Improved: HTML to Markdown converter (`TWTAEO_HTML_To_Markdown`),** tested against a Custom HTML homepage: every line is trimmed (4+ spaces of source indentation turned Custom HTML sections into Markdown code blocks) and whitespace-only lines collapse; `<table>` becomes a Markdown table (first row as header, rows padded, `|` escaped) instead of loose cells; tags inside a heading, cell, list item or link become spaces, so "AEO Ultimate" + "Best for AI Search" no longer run together; card links that wrap a heading and paragraphs flatten to one link; root-relative links become absolute; anchor-only links become plain text; `<pre>` keeps its exact text in a fenced block; list items no longer come out as a loose, blank-line list; `svg` and `template` are dropped. Links are converted before tables and headings, so a link inside a heading or table cell is kept (tested on a live site whose six guide cards lost their links); lines typed as bullet characters (•, ▪, ●, ◦) become Markdown list items. The same converter feeds Chunk View, the RAG content index, the OKF bundle and the MCP endpoint, which get the same fixes; Markdown tables use the separator row the chunker's `is_table()` expects.
* **Changed: Markdown response headers.** `nocache_headers()` (a page cache that ignores `Vary: Accept` could otherwise serve Markdown to a human visitor at the page's own address, and a cached copy hides AI-crawler fetches from the log), `Vary: Accept`, a content-hash `ETag` with `If-None-Match` → 304, `Last-Modified`, and `Link: rel="canonical"` to the HTML page so a `.md` address is never treated as duplicate content. Markdown is still built per request, never stored: rendered content also depends on shortcodes, reusable blocks and live product data that change without a save.
* **Fixed: password-protected posts** were returned in full as Markdown via `Accept: text/markdown` or `?aeo_format=markdown` (`the_content` was applied to the raw post content). `markdown_requested()` now refuses them, and their `.md` address 404s.
* **New: llms.txt option "Link each page to its Markdown version"** (`llms_md_links`, off by default; needs Markdown Content Negotiation).
* **New: crawler log records the format served** (`format`: `markdown` | `html`, from the same `markdown_requested()` the handler uses). AI Crawler Watch marks Markdown fetches; the `get-ai-crawler-activity` ability reports `markdown_hits`, and `/page.md` counts as a visit to the page in `get-uncrawled-pages`.
* **New bots: `Claude-User`, `Claude-SearchBot`, `Perplexity-User`** in the crawler log, the AI Ready bot registry (per-bot rules, user-agent verification), the rate limiter and the sitemap crawler list. They were not logged at all before: none contains an existing pattern.

* **Fixed: AI descriptions cut off mid-sentence (`TWTAEO_AI_Description::clean_description()`).** Models asked for 140–155 characters overshoot, and the 160-character limit then cut at the last whole word ("…ensure your content stands out with"), which search results and AI answers showed as written. Now the description ends at the last full sentence when one ends at 60 characters or later; otherwise it ends at a word, drops a trailing filler word ("with", "to", "the"…), and closes with "…".
* **New: "Cut off" flag on the Social Graph screen (`TWTAEO_AI_Description::looks_cut_off()`, `TWTAEO_OG_Detector` `cut_off`).** A description of 100+ characters that does not end like a sentence is marked in the Description column, with which one stops mid-sentence (og:description and/or the meta description from this plugin, Yoast, Rank Math, AIOSEO or SEOPress; templated values skipped), and a count above the table. Existing descriptions are flagged, never rewritten.

= 2.27.0 =

* **New: WordPress Abilities (`TWTAEO_Abilities`, `includes/class-abilities.php`).** Registered only when the Abilities API exists (WordPress 6.9+); older sites get nothing and no error. Category `aeo-ultimate`; five abilities, all `readonly`/`idempotent`, `show_in_rest` and `public` (so the MCP Adapter exposes them), permission `manage_options` (deliberately not `TWTAEO_Roles::CAP`, which is meant for the plugin's own screens, not REST clients holding an Application Password). Failures return a `WP_Error` with a Diagnostics reference (`TWTAEO_Logger::log_exception()`).
  * `get-ai-visibility-summary`: latest real run with answers (same selection as the RAG Engine): per engine cited / mentioned / not cited / unavailable counts and per-question results (up to 50). No full answer text.
  * `get-ai-crawler-activity` (`days` 1–30): hits by bot and company, last seen, top 10 pages, llms.txt / robots.txt hits, and the log's real window (500 entries; 30 days, or 48 hours when connected to the agency hub).
  * `get-uncrawled-pages` (`post_type`, `limit` 1–100): the newest 500 published, indexable pages of public post types, compared by permalink path against the crawler log.
  * `get-page-aeo-report` (`post_id` or `url`): the stored AEO Score breakdown, category totals and every not-fully-passing check with its reason and fix, largest loss first.
  * `get-content-gaps` (RAG Engine on): AI Citations opportunities and Next questions (document answers and "nothing answers these yet"), document titles only, never passage text.
* **Security (prompt injection).** Nothing an outsider wrote goes out raw: crawler URLs come from the request line, so they are reduced to a path and resolved to the site's own published posts (anything else is only counted); raw user agents, query strings and titles from the log are never returned; AI-predicted follow-up questions are flattened, capped at 200 characters and labelled as untrusted third-party text. Every list is capped and every value is read field by field, so no option or table row is returned whole.
* **Changed: llms.txt exclusions.** `maybe_serve_llms_txt()` over-fetches 40 posts/pages and keeps the first 20 that are not noindexed (`TWTAEO_Index_Heuristics::is_noindexed()`, now public: Yoast, Rank Math, SEOPress, AIOSEO v3 meta), not password-protected (`has_password => false`) and not the WooCommerce cart, checkout or my-account page. AIOSEO v4+ keeps noindex in its own table and is not read yet.

* **Bug fix (important, AI-Ready):** every plain-text/markdown endpoint served to AI crawlers — `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, `ai.txt`, Markdown content negotiation (`Accept: text/markdown`), and the Agent Skills documents — was passed through `wp_kses_post()` before output. That function is an HTML sanitizer: on non-HTML bodies it entity-encodes the text, so the llms.txt spec's required `> description` blockquote reached crawlers as `&gt; description`, every `&` as `&amp;`, and code fences in skill documents could be mangled. These responses are `text/plain`/`text/markdown` and cannot execute HTML, so escaping bought no safety and corrupted exactly the output the plugin exists to provide. All five endpoints now emit the raw document (UTF-8 validated). Found while testing 2.13.0 on a live site: its `llms.txt` literally began `&gt; AEO Dashboard`.
* **Efficiency:** the WooCommerce products table computed each product's image-alt summary twice per row; it is now computed once and reused.
* **Bug fix (WordPress 7, follow-up to the AI Client fix):** bulk AI social descriptions aborted with "Method using_max_output_tokens does not exist on WordPress\AiClient\Builders\PromptBuilder" after generating nothing. Two compounding traps: the WP wrapper serves every fluent method through `__call`, so `is_callable()` is true for **any** name — and calling an unsupported one does not throw; the wrapper **poisons the builder** and the eventual `generate_text()` returns `prompt_builder_error`, so try/catch cannot save it either. The plugin was calling `using_max_output_tokens`, which has never existed in the SDK (the real method is `usingMaxTokens`). Optional tweaks (model preference, token cap) are now gated on `method_exists()` against the underlying SDK class `WordPress\AiClient\Builders\PromptBuilder` — the only probe that reflects what the installed AI Client actually supports — and the cap uses the correct `using_max_tokens` name. Applied to both AI Client call paths (`TWTAEO_AI_Description` and `TWTAEO_AI_Client`).

* **Bug fix (important, WordPress 7 only):** the WordPress 7.0 AI Client path was dead. `WP_AI_Client_Prompt_Builder` declares only `__construct`, `using_abilities` and `__call` — every fluent method, `generate_text()` included, is served by `__call`, so `method_exists()` is **false** for all of them while `is_callable()` is true. Both guards used `method_exists()`, so `generate_text()` was **never called**: `TWTAEO_AI_Client::call_via_ai_client()` short-circuited to `''` and returned "returned no content" without making a request, and `TWTAEO_AI_Description::call_via_ai_client()` could only ever return "This WordPress AI Client version cannot generate text". Leaving the key fields blank — which the settings screen recommends on WordPress 7 — therefore disabled AI generation entirely. Now uses `is_callable()`, actually invokes the builder, and passes WordPress's own `WP_Error` message through instead of flattening it to a generic one. `TWTAEO_Rate_Limiter` had the same defect on `get_ability_name()`, logging `unknown` always; that one was cosmetic.
* **Feature:** `TWTAEO_Output_Control` — a switch for every output that could collide with another plugin, on the Schema Conflict Detector page. Nine keys: `schema_identity`, `schema_article`, `schema_service`, `schema_contact`, `schema_breadcrumb`, `llms_txt`, `sitemap`, `meta_description`, `crawler_log`. **Seven of these had no gate anywhere** — the Knowledge Graph spine, Article, Service and Contact writers, the AI meta description, AI-Ready's `/sitemap.xml` endpoint and the crawler logger all registered outside every module check, so a site running Yoast or Rank Math had duplicates with no lever short of deactivation. * **Note:** every key defaults to **on**, so updating changes the output of no existing install. Conflicts are detected and reported; nothing is ever switched off automatically, because silently removing schema from a working site during an auto-update is worse than a duplicate.
* **Note:** `llms_txt` and `schema_breadcrumb` **delegate** to the settings that already own them in `twtaeo_ai_ready` — read, never copied. `set()` refuses them and the admin shows those rows disabled with a link to the AI Ready screen. A second flag that can disagree with the first is the bug this shape exists to prevent.
* **Note:** ⚠️ switching an output off strips every reference into the removed nodes; a hand-off does not. They are not the same operation — a hand-off is guaranteed a same-`@id` replacement, a switch is not (Yoast addresses its WebPage by the bare URL, so references into `{url}#webpage` would orphan).
* **Improvement:** Surface hand-off to AEO Ultimate for WooCommerce. That plugin is a commerce-focused fork of this one, so the two published the *same* `@id`s — `home_url( '/#organization' )`, `home_url( '/#website' )` and `{url}#webpage` — both on `wp_head` priority 1, plus two `/llms.txt` writers, two `/sitemap.xml` writers, two meta description writers and two BreadcrumbLists. Article, News, Service, Author and PR nodes — which that plugin does not publish — are untouched, and references stay valid because the replacement carries the identical identifier.
